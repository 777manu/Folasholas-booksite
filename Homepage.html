They Grew: So Did I

Dear Parent: The Letters I Wish Someone Had Written to Me

Read Excerpt

Dear parent, I know how heavy the silence can feel at the end of a long day, when you're sitting on the edge of the bed, looking at a messy house and wondering if you're doing any of this right.

I know the quite grief of watching your peers climb professional ladders while you choose to step back, protect your time, and be present for your children.

This book is my gift to you.

It's a reminder that you don't have to be a perfect parent. You simply have to keep showing up, keep learning and keep loving.

And as you watch them develop, you may discover something beautiful: as they grew, you were growing alongside them too.
